# Source attribution

The official data, parser, prediction runner and scorer come from xudongwu-0/DASE7506-MP2, pinned in DATA_SOURCES.json. GSM8K attribution/MIT license and the supplied Qwen3-0.6B representation provenance are retained. No additional pretrained weights, external training data or candidate generations are used. AI_ASSISTANCE.md and the report's approved AI disclosure are unchanged.

The CE features, solver and answer-mean-pooling path reuse our earlier project implementation. Pooling was inspired by answer-group/output-pooling ideas in 325200wang/DASE7506-MP2 at 4de8e02e28e25048471389f94c3debd9fb8aca50, using our CE-softmax mean instead of that project's sigmoid pipeline. Earlier subset work drew on CaspianW/dase7506-mp2 at 6709b9ff3cfaea1fed0a4f897f6796358ccfcd02; it is not deployed.

The S28/V22 evidence definitions in code/v7_evidence.py are adapted from [Tianyi-325/DASE7506-MP2-main, code/math_evidence.py](https://github.com/Tianyi-325/DASE7506-MP2-main/blob/8737e0ab5257669e03b14ae6737f0e853c22ab2e/code/math_evidence.py), fixed commit 8737e0ab5257669e03b14ae6737f0e853c22ab2e. They are not claimed as independently invented rules. Our pre-fit synthetic/TRAIN-input checks corrected decimal question boundaries, scalar-unit and narrative-number extraction, terminal units and mathematical dollar delimiters. We integrated the official parser and input-only counts, fitted our own fixed-CE residual and kept our existing probability-mean-pooling decision. No external checkpoint was executed or imported.

The current R1 inference code and checkpoint are copied byte for byte from the pre-test-frozen standalone bundle. SOURCE_PROVENANCE.json records their hashes. FREEZE.json is explicitly a post-scoring packaging record. Original pre-test records remain unedited in evaluation/final_test; absolute paths there refer to their historical local context, not runtime dependencies.

Previous versions and published experimental implementations remain accessible through frozen-v1, frozen-v1.1 and frozen-v2. Current main contains only final inference, the selected fixed training recipe, the approved report and compact evidence. Historical results and the final four-question improvement are fixed-bank observations, not an untouched blind-test or significance claim.
